High confidence
  1. The Harp85 Causeway St · West End
  2. Howl at the Moon184 High St · Faneuil Hall / FiDi
  3. Scorpion Bar58 Seaport Blvd · same building as The Grand

It then loops back to The Harp. These were the same three stops in 2024 and 2025, and all three are 2026 venues.

Route 2 · Fenway → Theater District → Seaport

Medium confidence
  1. Loco Fenway61 Brookline Ave · Fenway
  2. Ghost Light Tavern253 Tremont St · the old Nash Bar stop, same address
  3. The Grand / Scorpion Bar (best guess)58 Seaport Blvd · 2025's Seaport stop, Empire, isn't in the crawl this year
  4. Rock Bottom115 Stuart St · Theater District

It then loops back to Loco Fenway. No past trolley served Back Bay.

Getting back to Fenway after Pauly D

If you leave The Grand by about 6, you might catch a last Route 2 loop to Loco Fenway. Don't count on it.

T, ~30–35 min: walk to South Station, take the Red Line to Park St, then the Green Line (B/C/D) to Copley for Back Bay or Kenmore for Fenway.

Uber/Lyft, 15–20 min: expect heavy Halloween surge pricing.

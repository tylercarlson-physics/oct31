Good to know

  • Free entry isn't closing time. The wristband gets you in free until each bar's cutoff. Be inside by then and you can stay. After it, expect a cover.
  • Big clubs may clear out after their window: The Grand, Big Night Live, The Greatest Bar, BSMNT.
  • Check your ticket's check-in spot. Mighty Squirrel is listed as crawl-only this year. Late check-in until 4: Game On, Big Night Live, Ned Devine's, Ghost Light, The Grand.
  • Trolleys are based on past years (about 2–6pm). The night-before email has the real map.
  • No Ubers. On the T, everyone taps their own card or phone. Trains run until about 1am.
